How an alert is confirmed

  1. A person receives a fall prediction above the confidence threshold.
  2. The same person must satisfy the consecutive-frame requirement.
  3. The cooldown prevents repeated alerts for that camera and person.

Missing tracks, low scores, and a gap longer than three seconds reset the streak.

Notification transport

Email is disabled initially. The default backend prints to the server console and is labelled console_only. Configure SMTP environment variables for real submissions.

SMS, mobile push, and physical buzzers are future integrations.
